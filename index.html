Research

My research lies at the intersection of Robotics, Computer Vision, and Machine Learning. I work on developing learning-based methods for manipulation with soft and hybrid robotic arms, focusing on dexterous control in cluttered and unstructured environments. I also work on tactile‑centric policies for manipulation for vision‑free exploration and object reconstruction for rigid arms and anthromorphic hands. This work finds use in many applications such as autonomous harvesting, human assistive devices, medical imaging, underwater exploration, manufacturing and more!

Shivani K. Kamtikar, S. Marri, B. T. Walt, N. K. Uppalapati, G. Krishnan, G. Chowdhary
